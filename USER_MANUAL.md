## 1. Getting Started
### Installing the App
1. Download the Safire APK (Android) or from your respective app store.
2. Install the application on your device.
3. Open **Safire** and enter your campus credentials to log in.

*(Note: Depending on your role, the application will automatically adjust to show you student features or administrator features).*

---

## 2. Mobile Application Features (For Users)

### Dashboard & Home
Upon logging in, you will land on the **Home Screen**. Here you can find quick-action buttons to directly access the most important tools like Reporting, Announcements, and the Campus Map. 

### Incident & Emergency Reporting
Safire makes it easy to quickly report campus issues or emergencies:
- **Standard Reporting (`ReportScreen`)**: Navigate to "File a Report", select the category (e.g., Maintenance, Security, Noise), add a description, and attach photos if necessary. 
- **My Reports (`MyReportsScreen`)**: Track the status of your submitted reports in real-time (Pending, In-Progress, Resolved).
- **Emergency Reporting (`EmergencyReportScreen`)**: Use the prominent red emergency button to immediately dispatch a priority alert to campus security.

### Announcements
- Check the **Announcements** tab to view official campus news, event updates, and critical alerts broadcasted by the administration.

### Campus Navigator & Hotspot Map
- **Campus Navigator**: Use this interactive map to find your way around campus buildings, classrooms, and facilities.
- **Hotspot Map**: View areas with reported high activity, ongoing maintenance, or temporary restrictions.

### Lost and Found
- Found something or lost a personal item? Navigate to **Lost and Found** to post a description and photo of the item, or browse the current list of found items to reclaim your property.

### Smart Attendance & Library
- **Smart Attendance**: Easily clock into classes or institutional events.
- **Library**: Browse library services, check study room availability, or access digital campus resources.

### Appeals System
- If you have an institutional decision that requires an appeal (e.g., false report strikes, academic appeals), use the **Appeals** screen to submit a formal request to the administrative committee.

### Live Chat & Support
- Need immediate assistance? Open the **Support/Chat Screen** to communicate directly with an available campus administrator or support agent.

## 3. Administrator Features (Web Panel & Admin Role)

Administrators have access to elevated capabilities on both the mobile app and the dedicated **Admin Web Panel**.

### Creating Announcements
*Only Administrators can create announcements.*
- **Mobile**: Click the `+` icon or "Create" button on the Announcements screen.
- **Web Panel**: Navigate to `Announcements Management` on the sidebar.
- Fill out the Title, Content, and priority level, then hit **Publish**. This will instantly notify all mobile app users.

### Managing Reports & Messaging
- Access the **Admin Dashboard** to get an overhead view of all incoming reports.
- **Admin Messaging**: Engage directly with users who have submitted tickets to request more details or provide updates on their report status.

### AI Moderation Settings
Safire utilizes an AI-based moderation system (Perspective API) to automatically filter out spam, harassment, and false reports.
- Administrators can tweak moderation strictness from the **Moderation Settings**.
- View automated moderation logs to ensure community guidelines are upheld.

## 5. Troubleshooting & Support

**Q: I found an issue with my app, or it crashes on startup.**
A: Ensure you have the latest updated version of the APK. Try clearing the app cache from your Android Settings -> Apps -> Safire -> Storage -> Clear Cache.

**Q: My report was marked as "Spam" automatically.**
A: Safire uses AI to filter inappropriate language or false reporting. If you believe this was an error, please use the **Appeals** screen to contact an administrator.

**Q: I cannot create an announcement.**
A: Only accounts with the `Admin` role authorized in the database can create announcements. If you are staff, request an admin role upgrade from the IT department.

*For further assistance, please use the in-app Support Chat or contact the campus IT helpdesk.*
